# Color System V2

The dominant surface remains white. Branding, navigation and interaction use:

- `--brand-900: #014BA0` — main header/navigation emphasis.
- `--brand-700: #0A5CB8` — primary controls and links.
- `--brand-500: #1466C3` — active tabs, focus and 3D selection.
- `--neutral-400: #C0C0C0` — strong neutral/disabled boundaries.
- `--neutral-200: #D9D5D5` — separators and subtle fills.
- `--surface-primary: #FFFFFF` — dominant background.

Green, red and amber remain reserved for PASS, FAIL and WARNING. REVIEW uses blue. Utilization colors retain their engineering meaning and are not replaced by corporate blues.
